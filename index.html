袁毓林教授研究团队主页 Homepage of Prof. YUAN Yulin's reasearch team

这里是袁毓林教授研究团队的学术主页，主要发布袁毓林教授及其研究团队在汉语语言学、计算语言学与相关领域的研究成果与学术进展。网站系统展示了团队成员自1980年代至今取得的各项科研成果，包括学术著作、期刊论文、会议发表、科研项目，以及在北京大学和澳门大学支持下开发的多项应用资源与技术评测任务。 Chinese Room is the academic homepage of Professor YUAN Yulin and his research team, introducing their research outputs and scholarly developments in Chinese linguistics, computational linguistics, and related fields. The website offers a systematic overview of the research accomplishments attained by the team members since 1980s, including academic monographs, journal articles, conference presentations, and research projects, as well as a range of application resources and shared tasks developed with the support of Peking University and the University of Macau.
