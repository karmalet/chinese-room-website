评测任务 Shared Tasks

本页面收录了由袁毓林教授研究团队发起或合作开展的技术评测任务。目前评测对象主要为大型语言模型。

This page presents the shared tasks initiated or jointly conducted by Prof. YUAN Yulin’s research team. At present, the primary focus of these tasks is large language model evaluation.

第二十五届中国计算语言学大会技术评测任务一 Shared Task 1 of the 25th China National Conference on Computational Linguistics (CCL 2026)

第二届中文叙实性推理评测

Factivity Inference Evaluation 2026 (FIE2026)

本任务聚焦中文叙实性推理能力评测，面向大语言模型与相关系统，关注模型在叙实性推理、事实判断与语义一致性方面的表现。
This task focuses on evaluating factivity inference in Chinese, targeting large language models and related systems, with special attention to their performance in factivity reasoning, factual judgment, and semantic consistency.
