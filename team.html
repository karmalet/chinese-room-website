个人简介 Resumé

袁毓林，男，1962年2月生，现任澳门大学讲座教授、澳门大学人文学院中国语言与文学系主任，曾任清华大学中文系副教授、北京大学中文系教授。 长期从事理论语言学和汉语语言学研究，并在计算语言学与中文信息处理方面持续推进交叉探索。

其研究兴趣包括（待老师补充）。。。

Yuan Yulin, male, was born in February 1962. He is currently Chair Professor at the University of Macau and Head of the Department of Chinese Language and Literature, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, University of Macau. He previously served as Associate Professor in the Chinese Department of Tsinghua University and as Professor in the Chinese Department of Peking University. He has long been engaged in research on theoretical linguistics and Chinese linguistics, while also continuing interdisciplinary exploration in computational linguistics and Chinese information processing.

His research interests includes ...

研究方向：理论语言学、汉语语言学、计算语言学、中文信息处理。

Research Fields：Theoretical Linguistics, Chinese Linguistics, Computational Linguistics, Chinese Information Processing
