袁毓林教授 Prof. YUAN Yulin

团队带头人 Primary Investigator

袁毓林，男，1962年生，澳门大学人文学院中国语言文学系讲座教授、系主任，曾任北京大学中文系教授、博士生导师。

Yuan Yulin, male, born in 1962, is Chair Professor and Head of the Department of Chinese Language and Literature, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, University of Macau. He previously served as Professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Chinese Language and Literature at Peking University.

研究方向：理论语言学、汉语语言学、计算语言学、中文信息处理

Research Interests: Theoretical Linguistics, Chinese Linguistics, Computational Linguistics, Chinese Language Information Processing

邮箱 Email: yulinyuan@um.edu.mo
